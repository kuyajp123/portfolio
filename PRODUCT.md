1. **Show, Don't Just Tell**: Every capability is backed by interactive demonstrations, verifiable links, real screenshots, and real-time live data.
2. **Editorial Precision Over Generic AI-Slop**: Clean typography, bespoke hairlines, thoughtful spacing, and distinct layout pacing.
3. **Frictionless Navigation**: Preserve per-page scroll states, provide clear back routes, and maintain instant responsiveness.
4. **Authentic Persona**: Seamlessly blend professional enterprise engineering with human presence (music, community, off-screen rituals).
